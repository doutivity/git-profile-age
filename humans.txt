    License: MIT
    Last update: 2026-09-13
    Language: English, Ukrainian
    Standards: HTML5, CSS3, ECMAScript 5 (vanilla JavaScript)

                  Gitee, GitCode, Hugging Face and selected self-hosted instances
    Architecture: single static index.html, no build step, no backend, no dependencies
    Privacy: no analytics, no tracking, no cookies
    Hosting: GitHub Pages
